Finding the right doctor

Remedium doesn’t rank or recommend named doctors. We can’t verify anyone’s results, and “top doctor” lists are often paid. Use these instead:

  • Check registration. Look the doctor up in the National Medical Commission register or your state medical council.
  • Book a free tele-consult with a government doctor on eSanjeevani.
  • Choose the right specialist using the table below, and get a second opinion before any major surgery or cancer treatment.
  • Ask about cost. Check if you qualify for schemes such as Ayushman Bharat before choosing a hospital.
